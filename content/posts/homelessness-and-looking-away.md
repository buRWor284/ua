![Homelessness](/images/homelessness-look-away.jpg)

Living in a city means walking past homeless people every day. At first it bothers you. Then, slowly, it doesn't — and that's the part that should bother you more.

There's a psychological term for this: *psychic numbing*. The more exposed we are to suffering, the less we feel it. It's a defense mechanism, but it's also how systemic problems stay invisible.

I'm not sure I have a solution to offer. I'm just trying to notice when I look away, and ask myself why.

---

If you want to help in a concrete way, look into local organizations doing direct service work — food, shelter, job training. Volunteering even occasionally keeps the problem from becoming abstract.
